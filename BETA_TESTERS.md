# PinBlocks RC beta

1. Download the build for your operating system.
2. Open PinBlocks. Your default browser should open automatically.
3. Choose a **copy or backed-up version** of your MPF game folder.
4. If you're new to MPF modes, use **Build Your First Mode**.
5. Report anywhere you did not know what to do, anything that did not behave as described, and whether the installed/exported mode ran in MPF.

The RC is intentionally not a complete visual editor for every MPF feature.
